Scope: lens.sirv.studio landing page (site/public/index.html), Persuade. Visitor: a developer, QA or product person who finds browser bugs and hands them on. Action: download Lens. Proof: real captures of the current extension on a demo store (site/screenshots.mjs). Constraints: self-hosted assets, CSP in _headers, manifest-driven downloads, light and dark, 390px phones.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the record of one bug, kept like a certified transcript: numbered lines, speakers, exhibits, redactions. It refuses the dark devtool hero with a glowing screenshot and the centred headline over three feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: Paper-white sheet and blue-black ink; a double hairline margin rule; Courier Prime for transcript lines, times and exhibit marks; Libre Franklin heavy for headings and plain for prose; brand blue as the exhibit sticker and the action; red only for failures; solid black redaction bars. No gray fills, no soft shadows: paper or ink, hairline edges. Raises: one line pitch drives the rhythm (generative identity); no midtones (Weimar bill); the chosen OS solid, the others dashed (sewing envelope); one crank, the transcript plays once in order (automata); fixed stations Record, Review, File (darkroom).

STORY: In one viewport the visitor reads what Lens keeps (what you did, what the page did, what you said, on one clock), then sees each claim as a numbered exhibit from the real app, learns what an agent gets, sees what is redacted, and downloads.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left, the H1 at display size, a two-line lede, Download (OS-aware) and How it works. Right, the transcript sheet: a case caption with the bug title, then six numbered lines (you said / step / 500 / screen) that type in once; an exhibit sticker links down to Exhibit A.

FORM: Court reporter's transcript with exhibits, candidate 3 of 7; seed key f66ca004.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
