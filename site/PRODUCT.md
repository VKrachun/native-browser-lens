## What it is

A free desktop app plus a browser extension (Chrome, Edge, Brave, Chromium, Firefox). The person
presses Record in the side panel, uses the site and talks. Lens keeps the clicks, keys, page
changes, network and console, the voice (transcribed locally with Whisper) and an rrweb replay.
Stop opens a review: title and findings, notes, marks and masks on shots, a replay with captions.
The review goes to Linear, GitHub Issues, Jira Cloud, Basecamp, VibeQ or the clipboard, or to a
coding agent (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, T3 Code, Zeron) through MCP and the `lens` CLI.

## Unique mechanism

One take holds what you did, what the page did and what you said, on one clock, and becomes a
record an agent can repeat.

## Audience

Web developers, QA people, designers and product people who find bugs in a browser and must hand
them to someone (a person or a coding agent). They read issue trackers, devtools, terminals and
diff views daily.

## Brand commitments

- Name: Sirv Lens. Icon: a blue rounded square with white viewfinder corners and a circle.
- Blue (#2b72ff to #0a4fe0) is the product color; the app follows Sirv's sid-kit tokens.
- Private by default: no Sirv server in the loop, secrets masked, replays offline by default.
- Free. No numbers, logos, customers or testimonials exist; never invent them.
