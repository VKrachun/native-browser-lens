Why Lens asks for each permission

Effective November 15, 2026

Lens has one purpose: to record a browser session with your voice and turn it into a bug report that can be reproduced. It reads a website only after you allow that website from its side panel, and everything it records stays on your computer until you file it. See the privacy policy.

Chrome, Edge, Brave and Chromium

PermissionWhy Lens needs it
debuggerTo see what the page did while you tested it: network requests and their status, the bodies of the site's own API calls and failed requests, console errors, and a screenshot after each step. Lens attaches only to tabs of sites you allowed, and Chrome shows its "started debugging this browser" bar while it does.
nativeMessagingTo talk to the Lens app on your computer, which stores recordings, writes down your voice, files tasks and serves coding agents. Lens talks to no server.
sidePanelLens works from the browser's side panel: Record, Stop, your recordings and their notes.
tabsTo know which site the active tab shows, so Lens collects only on sites you allowed, and to open the review page after a recording.
scriptingTo add the recorder to tabs of allowed sites. It records the page structure and your steps for the replay, with passwords and private fields masked.
activeTabTo capture the tab you are looking at when you use the Lens button or its keyboard shortcut.
storageTo remember your settings, such as Record or Capture mode and whether to record your voice.
offscreenTo keep recording your voice when you close the side panel during a recording. The microphone runs only while a recording with "Record my voice" is on.
faviconTo show each site's icon next to its name in the side panel.
Site access (optional): http://*/*, https://*/*Requested for one site at a time, when you choose "Collect evidence on this site". Lens has no access to any site until you grant it, and you can remove a site at any time in the browser's extension settings.

Firefox

PermissionWhy Lens needs it
webRequestFirefox has no debugger for extensions; Lens uses this to see the addresses, methods and status of the requests of allowed sites. It does not read request or response bodies in Firefox.
nativeMessaging, tabs, scripting, activeTab, storageAs in Chrome, above. Lens works from Firefox's sidebar.
Site access (optional): one site at a timeAs in Chrome, above.
Access to all websites (optional): <all_urls>Firefox lets an extension take a screenshot of a tab only with access to all websites, or right after you click its toolbar button. Lens asks for it when you first record, capture or collect, so it can take the screenshots of your recording. It still records only on the sites you added.

The microphone

The microphone is not an extension permission: the browser asks you the first time you record with "Record my voice" on. Lens transcribes the audio on your computer. You can include your recorded voice in an MP4 you save or attach to a Basecamp draft.
