Privacy policy

Effective November 15, 2026

In short: Sirv Lens runs on your computer. The publisher receives none of your data: Lens has no server and no analytics. What Lens records stays on your computer until you file or export it, and then it goes only where you send it.

Who we are

Sirv Lens ("Lens") is a browser extension and a companion app for your computer, published by Igor Varyvoda ("we"). For any question about privacy, write to lens@sirv.studio.

What Lens does

Lens has one purpose: to record a browser session with your voice and turn it into a bug report that a person or a coding agent can reproduce. Everything below serves that purpose and nothing else.

What Lens handles, and where it stays

Lens works only on websites you allow, one site at a time, from its side panel. On every other website it reads nothing.

When data leaves your computer

Only in these cases, and each one starts with an action of yours:

  • Opening a tracker draft. When you open a Linear or GitHub draft, the title and summary from your review travel in the URL and can appear in browser history. Lens copies longer text to your clipboard for pasting. For Jira Cloud, Lens copies the summary. For Basecamp, Lens fills the draft and starts uploading the files you select before you post the to-do. An MP4 includes recorded microphone audio unless you turn it off.
  • Filing to VibeQ. When you press "File to VibeQ", Lens sends the task (title, notes, findings, tags), the structured record, the screenshots and the page recording to the VibeQ address you configured, signed in as you. Before filing, Lens may search that VibeQ for similar tasks. If you choose "Put Sirvant to work", Lens asks your VibeQ to start its agent on the task. VibeQ is run by its own operator under its own terms.
  • Exporting. "Export" writes the same files to a folder on your computer. Where they go from there is up to you.
  • Your AI assistant, if you have one. If Claude Code or Codex is installed and signed in on your computer, Lens uses it to write titles and findings, to split long recordings into tasks and to check for duplicates. Lens passes it text only: your transcript, the steps, the page text, your notes and, for the duplicate check, the titles and descriptions of similar VibeQ tasks. The tool sends that text to Anthropic or OpenAI under your own account and their terms. No audio and no screenshots are sent for these. The separate "Draft the report" action, which you start yourself, also passes the screenshots. You choose the assistant in Settings; without one installed, nothing is sent.
  • Coding agents on your computer. If you connect your agents to Lens ("Connect coding agents" in Settings, or lens agents connect), Lens adds its skill and its MCP server (lens mcp) to the agents installed here: Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and Gemini CLI. They can then read the recordings you reviewed, and only those; they change nothing in Lens. "Disconnect" takes both back.
  • Handing a review off. When you press "Hand off" (or choose "Then hand off" before filing), Lens saves the review to a folder on your computer and opens the app you chose, Claude Code, Codex, T3 Code or Zeron, with a task that names the folder and the VibeQ task, or holds the report itself when the review was not filed. That app then works under your account and its own terms, and may send what it reads to its AI provider.
  • Downloads Lens makes. The app checks for updates and downloads signed updates from GitHub, and downloads the speech model from Hugging Face when you ask it to. These requests carry no recorded data; like any download, they reveal your IP address to those services.

What we never do

  • We do not collect, receive or store any of your data. Lens has no server, account or backend of ours.
  • Lens contains no analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, advertising or tracking.
  • We do not sell, rent, share or transfer user data to anyone, and we do not use it for any purpose unrelated to Lens's single purpose, for advertising, or to determine creditworthiness or for lending.
  • No person reads your recordings, because none of them ever reach us.

The use of information that Lens receives complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Permissions

Each browser permission Lens asks for, and why, is on the permissions page.

Keeping and deleting data

  • The extension keeps at most the last ten minutes of an allowed tab in memory. It is gone when the tab closes, when you stop collecting on the site, or when you quit the browser.
  • Recordings in the data folder stay until you delete them: delete an observation in the side panel, or remove the data folder after you uninstall the app.
  • To stop collection everywhere at once, use "Pause collection" in the Lens menu. To revoke a site, remove it in the browser's extension settings.
  • Tasks you filed stay in VibeQ; delete them there.

Security

Recordings are stored in your user account's data folder, protected by your operating system's file permissions. Your VibeQ token is kept in the system's secret store, not in a file. Lens talks to VibeQ over HTTPS only, and it installs only updates that carry our signature.

Children

Lens is a tool for people who build and test websites. It is not directed at children under 16.

This website

lens.sirv.studio is a static website. It uses no cookies and no analytics. It is hosted by Cloudflare, which processes requests, including IP addresses, to deliver and protect the site.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new effective date. A change that lets Lens send data anywhere new will be described here before it ships.
