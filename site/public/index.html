Show the bug. Say what's wrong. Hand it to an agent.

Lens records your browser session with your voice, then turns it into a task with the exact steps, the failed requests, the console errors and a replay. You file it; a coding agent can reproduce it.

Download Lens See how it works

Free. For Chrome, Edge, Brave and Firefox. Recordings stay on your computer until you file them.

From "this looks broken" to a task, in one take

No screenshots to annotate, no steps to type out. Use the site as you normally do and talk.

01 Record

Press Record and talk

Open the Lens side panel on the site you test and press Record. Lens keeps every click, key and page change, the network and the console, and your voice. In Chrome you can close the panel: the recording and your voice continue.

02 Review

Watch it back with your words

Press Stop and Lens opens the recording: a replay of the page with your voice and a transcript written on your computer.

  • Trim and cut what does not belong, draw on the page or highlight an element.
  • Black out anything private in a screenshot before it leaves.
  • A long recording with several problems becomes one task each.
03 File

File it, or put an agent on it

With your Claude Code or Codex, Lens writes the title and findings and checks VibeQ for the same problem. Then it files the task with the steps, the screenshots and the replay.

  • Hand it off in the same click: Claude Code, Codex, T3 Code or Zeron opens on the VibeQ task, in your project's folder.
  • Prefer files? Export it to a folder with a Playwright test.

What a coding agent gets

Every task carries one structured record. An agent reads it the way you would read the bug, and can repeat it.

  • Steps with real locatorsRole, name, test ID and CSS for each click and field, so a test can find the element again.
  • What the screen didThe text that appeared and went away after each click.
  • The failures that matterFailed requests with their bodies, linked to the step that caused them, and the console errors.
  • Your words, on the same clockWhat you said, where you said it, corrected if Whisper misheard.
  • A replay and screenshotsThe DOM recording and a shot after each step, with your masks burned in.

Working locally? One click in Settings, or lens agents connect, gives Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and Gemini CLI the Lens skill and MCP server: they find a review by its VibeQ task and read it straight from Lens. No MCP? lens review <id> and lens export <id> work from any shell.

Private by default

Lens is an app on your computer, not a cloud service. Nothing is uploaded to us, because there is no "us" in the loop.

You choose the sites

Lens reads a site only after you allow it, one site at a time. Everywhere else it does nothing.

Your voice stays here

Speech is written down by Whisper on your own computer. Audio never leaves it; only the text goes into a task you file.

Secrets are left out

Passwords, card numbers and private fields are masked. Cookies, headers and tokens are never recorded.

Nothing leaves until you file

Recordings stay on your computer. They go only where you send them: your tracker, or a folder.

Your own AI, if you want it

Titles and findings come from your Claude Code or Codex subscription, as text only. Turn it off by not installing either.

No tracking

No analytics, no telemetry, no ads. This website does not use cookies either.

Questions

Is Lens free?

Yes.

Why is there an app and an extension?

The extension records the page. The app keeps the recordings on your computer, writes down your voice with Whisper, files tasks and serves recordings to coding agents. Browsers do not let an extension do all of that on its own.

Which browsers work?

Chrome, Edge, Brave and Chromium, with network bodies and screenshots after each step. Firefox works too, with request details limited by what Firefox lets extensions see.

Where do tasks go?

Today Lens files to VibeQ, and exports any recording to a folder with a report, the screenshots, the record and a Playwright test. More trackers are planned.

Do I need an AI subscription?

No. With Claude Code or Codex installed and signed in, Lens writes titles and findings, splits long recordings and checks for duplicates. Without them you write the title yourself; everything else works.

Does anything go to Sirv?

No. Lens has no server of its own. See the privacy policy.
