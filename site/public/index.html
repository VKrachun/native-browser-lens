Show the bug. Say what's wrong. Hand it to an agent.

Lens records your browser session with your voice. It keeps what you did, what the page did and what you said on one clock, and turns it into a task with the steps, the failed requests, the console errors and a replay.

Download Lens How it works

Free. For Chrome, Edge, Brave and Firefox, on macOS, Windows and Linux. Recordings stay on your computer until you file them.

From “this looks broken” to a task, in one take

No steps to type out and no screenshots to crop. Use the site as you normally do and talk. These are the real screens, recorded on a demo store.

Press Record and talk

Open the Lens side panel on the site you test and press Record. Lens keeps every click, key and page change, the network and the console, and your voice.

In Chrome you can close the panel: the recording and your voice continue.

Watch it back with your words

Press Stop and Lens opens the recording: a replay of the page, your voice as captions, and a transcript written on your computer next to the steps and errors of the same second.

  • Cut what does not belong, draw on the page or point at an element, and add a note.
  • Mark up a screenshot and black out anything private before it leaves.
  • A long recording with several problems becomes one task each.

File it, or put an agent on it

Lens writes a title and findings with your Claude Code or Codex, if you have one. Edit them, then take the review to Linear, Jira Cloud, Basecamp, or GitHub Issues. You choose when to submit the task.

  • Hand the review to Claude Code, Codex, T3 Code or Zeron in your project’s folder.
  • Prefer files? Export it to a folder with a Playwright test.

What a coding agent gets

Each exported capture has a structured record. An agent reads it the way you would read the bug, and can repeat it.

Steps with real locators
Role, name, test ID and CSS for each click and field, so a test can find the element again.
What the screen did
The text that appeared and went away after each click.
The failures that matter
Failed requests with their bodies, linked to the step that caused them, and the console errors.
Your words, on the same clock
What you said, where you said it, corrected if Whisper misheard.
A replay and screenshots
The DOM recording and a shot after each step, with your masks burned in.

Working locally? One click in Settings, or lens agents connect, gives Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and Gemini CLI the Lens skill and MCP server: they find a review and read it straight from Lens. No MCP? lens review <id> and lens export <id> work from any shell.

Private by default

Lens is an app on your computer, not a cloud service. Nothing goes to Sirv: there is no Sirv server in the loop.

You choose the sites
Lens reads a site only after you allow it, one site at a time. Everywhere else it does nothing.
Local transcription
Whisper transcribes your voice on your computer. You choose whether to include microphone audio in a replay video you share.
Secrets are left out
Passwords, card numbers and private fields are masked. Cookies, headers and tokens are never recorded.
Replays stay offline
A replay loads nothing from the recorded site unless you ask it to show the site’s images.
Nothing leaves until you file
Recordings stay on your computer. They go only where you send them: your tracker, an agent or a folder.
No tracking
No analytics, no telemetry, no ads. This website does not use cookies either.

The details: privacy policy and why Lens asks for each permission.

Download Lens

Install the app and open it once. It walks you through adding the browser extension, and installs for you only, without administrator rights.

macOS

Apple silicon and Intel. Signed and notarized by Apple.

Windows

Windows 10 and 11, x64. Installs for you only, without administrator rights.

The installer is not code-signed yet. If Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”, choose More info, then Run anyway.

Linux

x86-64. AppImage for any distribution, or a package.

Every file and its SHA-256 sum is also on GitHub Releases.

The browser extension

Chrome, Edge and Brave: add Sirv Lens from the Chrome Web Store. Firefox: add Sirv Lens from Firefox Add-ons. The app opens these links for you after you install it.

Questions

Q.Is Lens free?

A.Yes.

Q.Why is there an app and an extension?

A.The extension records the page. The app keeps the recordings on your computer, writes down your voice with Whisper, files tasks and serves recordings to coding agents. Browsers do not let an extension do all of that on its own.

Q.Which browsers work?

A.Chrome, Edge, Brave and Chromium, with network bodies and screenshots after each step. Firefox works too, with request details limited by what Firefox lets extensions see.

Q.Where do tasks go?

A.Open a draft in Linear or GitHub Issues, or copy the summary into a Jira Cloud issue or Basecamp to-do. Use Clipboard for another tracker. Export to Downloads gives you the full evidence to attach.

Q.Do I need an AI subscription?

A.No. With Claude Code or Codex installed and signed in, Lens writes titles and findings, splits long recordings and checks for duplicates. Without them you write the title yourself; everything else works.

Q.Does anything go to Sirv?

A.No. Lens has no server of its own. See the privacy policy.
