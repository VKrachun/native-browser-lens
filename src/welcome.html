Chrome, Edge, Brave or Chromium

  1. Open Sirv Lens in the Chrome Web Store and choose Add to Chrome. Edge first asks to allow extensions from other stores.
  2. Pin Lens: click the puzzle piece in the toolbar, then the pin next to Sirv Lens.

No store? Load Lens from this computer:

  1. Open the extensions page. Paste this address into the address bar (Edge: edge://extensions, Brave: brave://extensions):
    chrome://extensions
  2. Turn on Developer mode, at the top right of that page.
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose this folder:
    {{CHROME_FOLDER}}

Firefox

  1. Open Sirv Lens in Firefox Add-ons and choose Add to Firefox.

No store? Load Lens from this computer, for one session at a time:

  1. Open this address:
    about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox
  2. Click Load Temporary Add-on… and choose this file:
    {{FIREFOX_MANIFEST}}

After Firefox restarts, load it again the same way.

Then record your first defect

Open the site you test and click the Lens button. Choose Collect evidence on this site, press Record, use the site and say what you see. Press Stop: Lens opens the recording with your words, ready to cut, annotate and file.

Recordings stay on this computer until you file them. To see this page again, open Sirv Lens from your applications.
